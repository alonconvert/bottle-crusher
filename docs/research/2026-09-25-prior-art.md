# Prior art: who else has tried to crush bottles

Date: 2026-09-25. Found through web searches. Several sites (Instructables and
some shops) blocked direct page reading, so some notes come from search
summaries. Nothing here was verified hands-on. No prices checked.

## 1. DIY and 3D-printed projects

- **Crank and gear water bottle crusher** (Instructables, a student CAD
  project). A crank turns gears that drive a press plate along two guide
  rails. PETG for the gears and the plate, PLA for everything else. It is the
  closest thing to our project. It looks like a plain class project with no
  design ambition.
  https://www.instructables.com/Water-Bottle-Crusher-1/
- **Universal Bottle Crusher** (Instructables). Gravity: a rope pulls a weight
  up a 4-inch PVC pipe and drops it on the bottle. Crude, but it shows that
  gravity and a falling weight are a possible "carrier".
  https://www.instructables.com/Universal-Bottle-Crusher/
- **Printed can crushers** (Printables, MakerWorld). Many of them, lever-based,
  some press-fit with no screws. They are made for cans, not for 1.5 L PET.
  https://www.printables.com/model/29713-simple-can-crusher ,
  https://makerworld.com/en/models/613736-can-crusher-15cl
- **The makers mostly cut bottles instead of crushing them.** The popular
  PET projects cut bottles into strips to make 3D-printer filament or rope:
  Recreator 3D, RePET, Thingiverse bottle cutters.
  https://www.printables.com/model/154015-the-recreator-3d-community-bottle-cutter-recycle-p ,
  https://github.com/ecodecat3d/RePET

**Takeaway:** there is no well-designed, desirable PET crusher in the maker
world. That is a gap we could fill.

## 2. Products you can buy

- **Wall-mounted lever crushers.** Mostly for cans. Where they take PET, it is
  only up to about 600 ml, and they claim up to 80% volume reduction.
  https://www.amazon.com/KitchenCraft-Crusher-Bottle-Opener-Metal/dp/B0001IWWIY
- **Japan has a whole category** called ペットボトルつぶし器 ("PET bottle
  squashers"): electric, hand-pressed, and step-on types. Reviews say a
  crushed PET bottle ends up about **1/2 to 1/4** of its original size.
  https://360life.shinyusha.co.jp/articles/-/18415
- **Commercial benchtop "safety baler"** (plasticbottlecrusher.com, Model
  6000). Takes bottles up to 2.25 L, for kitchens and workplaces.
  https://plasticbottlecrusher.com/model-6000-safety-baler-for-kitchen-benchtop/
- **Thailand:** wall-mounted crushers selling for a few hundred baht are
  reviewed on Lemon8; a Toyota dealer group (Phithan Toyota) has a bottle
  crusher project; a Thai shop sells a hydraulic press for bottles; and Thai
  school and invention projects exist. One Thai tip: loosen or remove the cap
  before crushing so the bottle does not bounce back.
  https://www.lemon8-app.com/@pkgoaround/7246756589695140354?region=us ,
  https://www.phithan-toyota.com/th/plastic_bottle_crusher ,
  https://www.yoryonghahheng.com/17332930/

**Takeaway:** everything on the market gets about 1/2 to 1/4. Our goal of a
tight puck at about 1/6 to 1/7 of the original volume (see
`01-requirements.md`) would clearly beat them, if it can be reached.

## 3. How the big machines do it

- **Reverse vending machines** (the machines that take bottles back for a
  deposit, e.g. TOMRA). They squeeze bottles between two moving belts in a
  V-shaped funnel, often with studs that pierce the bottle. Result: about 2.5
  times smaller. Their known trouble spots are **the neck with the cap** and
  **the thick dimple in the bottom of the bottle**, which resist crushing.
  https://www.tomra.com/reverse-vending/media-center/feature-articles/inside-reverse-vending-machines

**Takeaway:** the neck and the base are the hard parts everywhere. Our design
leaves the neck sticking out, so the base is the part to solve.

## 4. Bottles designed to be crushed

- **Evian's crushable bottle** (patents from the 1990s). Rings around the body,
  each with small "fold starters" offset from ring to ring, so the empty
  bottle folds into a neat accordion with **less than 100 N** of force, about
  10 kg.
  https://patents.google.com/patent/WO1996022918A1/en ,
  https://patents.google.com/patent/US5632397A/en
- **Ordinary bottles** take about 200 to 400 N before they first buckle. That
  is the range used in bottle quality testing, not the full crush.
  https://www.mecmesin.com/publications/top-load-testing-pet-bottles
- **Kresling origami** (research). When a thin tube is twisted, it folds into
  angled triangles that collapse very compactly with only moderate bending of
  the plastic.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.10411

**Takeaway:** a bottle folds neatly and easily when something tells it
*where* to fold. Evian builds the fold lines into the bottle. We could build
them into the crusher instead: ribs, a twist, or a shaped chamber that makes
an ordinary Chang or Singha bottle fold like an Evian one. That is also how we
could get a star or a spiral shape.

## 5. A different way to look at it

- **Precious Plastic** (open-source recycling machines): shred, melt, and
  press plastic into sheets using a hydraulic jack. It needs heat and
  ventilation, so it is not for a kitchen, but it shows the "turn waste into
  material" mindset.
  https://www.onearmy.earth/news/sheetpress
- **Bottle-to-filament** projects: the crusher's printed parts could, in
  theory, be printed from filament made from the household's own bottles. That
  would make a good story for a brand.
