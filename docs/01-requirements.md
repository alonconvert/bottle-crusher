# Requirements

Status: **draft, waiting for the owner's answers.**

## What we know

- Crushes empty 1.5 L PET drinking bottles.
- Lives on a kitchen counter in Thailand, so it faces heat and humidity.
- Parts come from online fabrication services and 3D printing.

## Open questions for the owner

### The bottles
1. Which brands do you drink most? Photos of 2 or 3 empty ones help.
2. Is the cap on or off when you crush them? Do you want to keep the cap?
3. Should it handle other sizes too, like 600 ml or 350 ml?

### The result
4. How flat is flat enough? A rough target: 1.5 L bottle down to under 60 mm.
5. Crushed along its length, like a can, or flattened sideways?
6. Where do crushed bottles go: fall into a bin, or you pick them out?

### Use
7. How many bottles a day or week?
8. Hand lever, foot pedal, or motor? Manual is simpler, cheaper, and safer.
9. Wall-mounted, or free-standing on the counter?
10. Maximum footprint on the counter, in centimetres?

### Look and feel
11. Should it look like a kitchen appliance, or is a workshop look fine?
12. Colour or material preferences: wood, steel, aluminium, plastic?

### Budget and tools
13. Total budget for the finished device, in baht?
14. Do you own or have access to a 3D printer?
15. Which basic tools do you have: screwdrivers, hex keys, a drill?
