# 003: No special features for a forgotten cap

Date: 2026-09-25
Status: accepted

## Question

Decision 001 said the crusher must stay safe if someone forgets to take the cap
off. How much design effort goes into that?

## Options considered

1. **Active protection.** For example, a spike that pierces the bottle to let
   the air out, a sensor, or a shape that will not close on a capped bottle.
   This adds parts, cost, and complexity. A spike also stops the "cap back on"
   trick from working, because air gets back in through the hole.
2. **No special features. Rely on the enclosed crushing chamber.** The chamber
   already has to be enclosed to keep fingers out (R7). If a capped bottle ever
   splits or pops its cap, that happens inside the box, so it makes a mess but
   does not hit anyone.

## Decision

Option 2. Only the owner and one other adult use the crusher, and they will
always take the cap off.

## Why

The owner guarantees cap-off use in this household. The enclosed chamber is
needed anyway for finger safety. It also meets the project safety rule of "no
spraying, no breaking" at no extra cost, so a mistake means wiping out the
chamber, not an injury.

## Revisit when

The crusher is designed for sale. Buyers will forget the cap, so active
protection would need a second look then.
